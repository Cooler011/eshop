Classification

BBFC Information Page

In the UK, interactive games will in most cases be rated under the PEGI system. However, some games may require rating by the British Board of Film Classification and will therefore be given a BBFC rating instead of the PEGI rating.

The British Board of Film Classification was established in 1912. It now rates cinema films, home video, and may also rate certain video content in interactive games.

The BBFC categories are as follows:

Universal

Suitable for all

Parental Guidance

General viewing, but some scenes may be unsuitable for young children

12

Suitable for those aged 12 and over

15

Suitable for those aged 15 and over

18

Suitable for those aged 18 and over

For more information, please refer to the BBFC website at www.bbfc.co.uk.
