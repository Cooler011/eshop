About Nintendo eShop

This version of Nintendo eShop is offered for the
European/Australian version of the Wii U console.

Nintendo eShop offers paid and free downloads of Nintendo
and third-party software as well as information about Nintendo and
third-party products and services for your Wii U console.

Nintendo eShop also allows you to manage funds registered
to your Nintendo Network ID and to redownload software to
the Wii U console.

Please note that all your paid and free downloads as well as all
funds are tied to your Nintendo Network ID. You will be unable
to use any purchased and free downloads of software and any
funds if you delete your Nintendo Network ID. If you delete your
Nintendo Network ID, all paid and free downloads and all funds
will be deleted, any purchased and free downloads stored on a USB
storage device will no longer function and you will no longer be able
to redownload previously purchased software.

In addition to the information offered within Nintendo eShop,
you can choose to receive notifications on the Wii U Menu.
These notifications may include commercial information such as
marketing or advertising messages and updates about products
and services offered by Nintendo and third parties (you can opt
out of receiving such notifications any time).

Information about the way you use Nintendo eShop, including
information about your Nintendo eShop page-viewing history,
purchase history, titles added to your Watch List and your reviews,
will be processed by Nintendo and its affiliates. Nintendo will use
such information for improving descriptions of software so that other
customers can make better judgements before purchase, for
developing new products and for improving the service. Further, if
you are at least 16 years old Nintendo will use such information for
customising recommendations and offers on Nintendo eShop to
better meet your interests. Please see the Nintendo Network Privacy
Policy for more information.

Nintendo eShop is subject to the Nintendo Network
Agreement and Nintendo Network Privacy Policy.
You can view the Nintendo Network Agreement and
Nintendo Network Privacy Policy on your Wii U console
(User Settings ⇒ Nintendo Network Agreement and Privacy Policy)
or on the Nintendo website: http://ms.nintendo-europe.com/terms/.
